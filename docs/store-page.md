## 3 icon concepts

The icon is seen tiny, so one bold shape, high contrast, almost no text.

1. **The glowing fist-crown.** A chunky cartoon figure silhouette, arms raised, with a bright aura ring behind
   it in one faction colour on a dark background. Focal point: the glowing ring.
2. **Four colours, one zone.** A top-down glowing circle (the zone) split into four colour wedges, with a tiny
   figure in the middle. Reads as "territory fight" instantly and shows all four factions.
3. **Face-off.** Two cartoon fighters nose to nose, aura sparks clashing between them, each in a different
   colour. Expressive faces, comic style, nothing mean-spirited.

## 3 thumbnail concepts (16:9, 1920 x 1080)

Use real in-game screenshots (or art faithful to the game). No fake buttons, arrows or UI.

1. **Capture moment.** A wide shot of a zone mid-capture: teammates standing in the ring, the capture bar half
   full, a rival being shoved back. Short text only if large: "CAPTURE THE ZONE".
2. **Mog-off.** Close, dramatic two-player tapping duel with a big countdown "5" and sparks. Shows the signature
   mechanic. Text: "WIN THE MOG-OFF" (only if the tone stays playful).
3. **Four-faction map.** A top-down view of the map with four coloured bases and a glowing centre zone, players
   rushing in from every side. Text: "4 FACTIONS. 1 MAP."

Tips: faces and bright colours draw clicks; keep the focal point in the centre; make it readable at phone size;
show the real game so players are not misled.

>
> **Fair play:** everything that changes how strong you are is earned in the game. Optional purchases are cosmetic
> only (trails, auras and titles).
>
> Short wars, easy to learn, made for playing with friends.
